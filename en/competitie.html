FISM Contest

The International Competition

FISM Qualified contest - open to all magicians, amateur or professional, with no age limit.

Enter the competition that can take you to the FISM European or World Championship! MagicArt Fest gives magicians the opportunity to compete in a FISM Qualified Contest, before a prestigious international jury.

The competition is open in the Stage, Close-Up, Mentalism and Junior categories, and is aimed at artists who want to prove their talent, gain international recognition and begin their journey towards the highest stage of world magic.

Take on the challenge and let your magic inspire the world!

01

Stage Magic

Stage magic with spectacular visual effects for a wide audience.

02

Close-Up Magic

Close-up magic, tricks with cards, coins and everyday objects.

03

Mentalism

Mind reading, predictions and high-impact psychological effects.

04

Junior

The special category for young magicians under 16.

Competition Rules

  • The competition is open to all magicians, professional or amateur, with no age limit.
  • Acts must last between 5 and 10 minutes.
  • The international jury judges: stage presence, technique and presentation, originality and creativity.
  • Competition categories: Stage Magic, Close-Up Magic, Mentalism and Junior (under 16).

Awards

The Festival Trophy, category prizes, the “Most Original Act” prize and special jury prizes.
