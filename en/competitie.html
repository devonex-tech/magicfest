FISM Contest

Magic Competition 2027 - FISM Qualifier in Romania

A FISM Qualified stage magic contest in Europe, open to all magicians, amateur or professional, with no age limit.

Enter the competition that can take you to the FISM European or World Championship! MagicArt Fest gives magicians the opportunity to compete in a FISM Qualified Contest, before a prestigious international jury.

The competition is open in the Stage, Close-Up, Mentalism and Junior categories, and is aimed at artists who want to prove their talent, gain international recognition and begin their journey towards the highest stage of world magic.

Competition Rules

1. Eligibility

  • The competition is open to all magicians, professional or amateur, with no age limit.
  • The number of contestants is limited. Entry is based on the application form completed and sent to the organizers before the deadline.
  • A contestant awarded at a previous edition may not compete with the same act or a very similar version of it.

2. Judging

  • The jury is made up of members recognized internationally in the field of magic.
  • The jury’s decisions are final and cannot be appealed.
  • In the event of a tie, the jury president has the deciding vote.
  • Stage presence - attitude, stage movement, the character created and how well it fits the theme of the routine.
  • Technique and presentation - level of difficulty, correctness of execution and the handling of classic techniques.
  • Originality and creativity.

3. Competition Categories

  • Stage Magic - manipulation, general magic, illusions, comedy magic.
  • Close-Up Magic - close-up work with cards, coins and everyday objects.
  • Mentalism - mind reading, predictions and psychological effects.
  • Junior - for participants under 16, in stage or close-up magic.
  • The jury reserves the right to move a contestant to a different category where justified.

4. Rules for Contestants

  • The act must run between 5 and 10 minutes.
  • The contestant must present a complete routine, not a single trick.
  • Obvious copying of an existing act results in disqualification.
  • Contestants must be ready at least 10 minutes before their scheduled time.
  • Exceeding the maximum time results in disqualification.
  • Each contestant completes the technical form (lights, music, props) and sends it to the organizers within the deadline communicated after their entry is confirmed.

5. Awards

  • 1st Place - the Festival Trophy.
  • 2nd and 3rd Place.
  • Audience Award - most original act.
  • Special prizes from the jury and the organizers.

How to enter the magic competition

The contest is part of MagicArt Fest 2027, the 2nd edition of the first FISM accredited magic festival in Romania, organised by Asociația Magic Art at the "Elisabeta Bostan" House of Culture in Buhuși, Bacău county, on 7-8 May 2027. Entry is by registration, while places last.

The Standard package costs 119 euro and the Full package 149 euro. Both include access to the Close-Up and Stage contest, the Dealer's Area, the International Gala Show, the festive dinner and the Magic Party, while the Full package adds a goody bag and 4 lectures. Accommodation at partner hotels is optional, from 30 euro per night per person in a double room.

Discounts: 10% for FISM card holders (code FISM10), 20% for participants under 18 (code UNDER18) and a 5% loyalty discount for participants of the 2026 edition.
