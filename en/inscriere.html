Available discounts

  • FISM card holders get 10% off, with the code FISM10.
  • Participants under 18 get 20% off, with the code UNDER18.
  • Participants of the 2026 edition get a 5% loyalty discount, applied when the registration is confirmed.

Accommodation (optional)

If you wish to stay at one of the MagicArt Fest partner hotels, tick the option in the form. The organisers will then send you the available hotels, rates and booking details.

  • 30 € / night / person - place in a double room, at the partner hotels.
  • A single room involves a higher cost. The final rate is confirmed together with your registration.
