Are you a magician looking to take part in the festival? Access to the Gala Show is already included in the Standard and Full packages. See the registration packages.

What the ticket includes

With every ticket

  • Access to the International Gala Show, the magic show of the festival.
  • A seat in the hall, according to the ticket category.
  • Access to the Magic Shop.

With the VIP ticket you also get

  • A seat in the front rows and priority access to the hall.
  • A meet and greet with photos with the artists after the show.
  • A MagicArt Fest surprise gift.

When and where

  • 7-8 May 2027, the 2nd edition of MagicArt Fest.
  • "Elisabeta Bostan" House of Culture, Buhuși, Bacău county, Romania.
  • Recommended for ages 6 and over.

Before you book

See the gala show programme to find out what happens on stage and who performed at the first edition. If you would rather compete than watch, read the international magic competition rules and then register for MagicArt Fest 2027.

Early Bird prices are available for a limited time; the full price is 89, 129 and 169 lei respectively. For bookings and details you can always write to us through the contact page.
