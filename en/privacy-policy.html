Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect through magicartfest.eu, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what rights you have over it. It is written in plain language and follows Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the applicable Romanian law.

1. Who the data controller is

The controller is Asociatia Magic Art (A.M.A.), the organiser of the MagicArt Fest International Magic Festival, an association founded in Romania in 2005.

How to reach us about anything related to personal data:

  • Email: festmagic@yahoo.com
  • Phone and WhatsApp: +40 729 290 290
  • Festival venue: Strada Republicii, Buhusi 605100, Bacau county, Romania

The association is not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer under Article 37 GDPR. Please use the email address above for any request concerning your data.

2. What we collect and why

2.1. Magician registration for the festival

When you fill in the registration form we collect: first and last name, stage name, email address, WhatsApp number, country, magic society (if you mention one), the package you choose (Magician, Competitor, Dealer or Dealer Assistant), the accommodation option and the name of the person you wish to share a room with, the competition category (Stage, Close-Up or no competition entry), the dealers' area option together with your store name and website, and any comments you write in the form.

We use this data to record your participation, reserve your place and accommodation, organise the competition by category, issue your participation documents and communicate with you about the festival.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR - performance of the participation contract to which you are a party, and the pre-contractual steps you requested.

2.2. Online payment

Card payments are processed entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number, expiry date or CVV. From Stripe we only receive the payment confirmation, the amount, the currency and a transaction identifier, which we link to your registration.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for processing the payment and Article 6(1)(c) for our accounting and tax obligations.

2.3. Newsletter

If you subscribe to the newsletter we collect your email address, your consent tick, the language of the page you subscribed from and the time of subscription. We use them to send you news about the next edition: guests, prices and Early Bird offers.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(a) GDPR - your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link in every message or by writing to us.

2.4. Messages sent by email, phone or WhatsApp

When you contact us directly we process the data you choose to give us (name, contact details, the content of your message), strictly in order to reply.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR - our legitimate interest in answering people who write to us, or Article 6(1)(b) when the message concerns a registration or a ticket.

2.5. Website usage statistics

Only after you explicitly accept the "Analytics and statistics" category in the cookie banner do we load Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity. These process your IP address (truncated by Google), device and browser type, the pages you visit, where you came from and, in the case of Clarity, an anonymised recording of your interactions with the page (mouse movement, scrolling, clicks). The purpose is purely statistical: to understand what works on the site and what needs improving.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Article 4(5) of Romanian Law no. 506/2004 - your consent. If you decline, these tools are not loaded at all. Full details are in our Cookie Policy.

2.6. Website security

Our hosting provider automatically records technical logs (IP address, time of the request, page requested, browser type) which are necessary to run the service and to prevent abuse. Our forms also include a hidden anti-spam field.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR - our legitimate interest in keeping the website available and secure.

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone. We share it only with the service providers who help us run the festival, each acting as a processor under a data processing agreement:

4. Transfers outside the European Union

Some of the providers above are based in, or have infrastructure in, the United States. In those cases the transfer takes place only under the safeguards set out in Chapter V GDPR: the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission, or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified providers. You can ask us by email at any time for further information about the safeguards that apply.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Festival registration data: for the duration of the edition concerned and afterwards for as long as needed to manage our relationship with participants and any complaints.
  • Payment records: for the periods required by Romanian accounting and tax law.
  • Newsletter email address: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.
  • Messages received by email or WhatsApp: for as long as needed to handle your request, plus a reasonable period afterwards.
  • Google Analytics 4 statistics: up to 14 months. Microsoft Clarity data: approximately one year.
  • Your cookie choice: stored locally in your browser until you change it or clear the site data.

6. Your rights

As a data subject you have the following rights:

  • Right of access - to find out whether we process data about you and to receive a copy of it.
  • Right to rectification - to correct inaccurate data or complete incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure (the "right to be forgotten"), under the conditions set out in law.
  • Right to restriction of processing in the situations listed in Article 18 GDPR.
  • Right to data portability - to receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.
  • Right not to be subject to automated decision-making with significant effects. We do not take such decisions and we do not build automated profiles.

You can exercise any of these rights by writing to festmagic@yahoo.com. We reply within one month of receiving your request. We may ask you for additional information in order to confirm your identity.

7. Complaint to the supervisory authority

If you believe we have breached your rights, please write to us first so we can put things right. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority:

Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP)

  • Address: B-dul General Gheorghe Magheru no. 28-30, sector 1, postal code 010336, Bucharest, Romania
  • Phone: +40 318 059 211 / +40 318 059 212
  • Email: anspdcp@dataprotection.ro
  • Website: www.dataprotection.ro

You also have the right to bring the matter before the competent courts.

8. Data security

The website runs exclusively over HTTPS. We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure: restricted database access, access keys kept in environment variables, security headers at server level and a strict policy on the content sources the site is allowed to load.

9. Children's data

The festival is open to the general public and entrants under 18 may take part in the competition. In those cases the registration and the payment must be made by a parent or legal guardian, who also consents to the processing of the minor's data. If you become aware that a minor has sent us data without that consent, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the services we use or the legal requirements change. The version in force is always the one published on this page, and the date of the last update is shown at the top. If the changes are significant we will let you know through the newsletter or with a notice on the website.
