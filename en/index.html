What is MagicArt Fest?

Experience the magic and thrill of an electrifying show, unprecedented in Romania. MagicArt Fest brings together the world's most talented magicians in an unforgettable spectacle.

Organized by the Magic Art Association (A.M.A.) since 2005, the festival is a FISM Qualified contest – the world's most prestigious magic organization. For three days, Buhuși becomes the capital of European magic with international competitions, a gala show featuring world-class artists, interactive workshops and moments of pure fascination.
