What is MagicArt Fest?

Experience the magic and thrill of a festival unlike anything Romania has seen before. MagicArt Fest brings together some of the most talented magicians on the international scene for an unforgettable experience.

Now in its second edition, MagicArt Fest is organized by the Magic Art Association (A.M.A.), founded in 2005. It is Romania's only FISM accredited magic festival (Fédération Internationale des Sociétés Magiques), the most prestigious magic organization in the world. The 2027 edition takes place on 7 and 8 May at the “Elisabeta Bostan” House of Culture in Buhuși, Bacău county, following the first edition held on 5-7 February 2026. The programme brings together the international magic competition with Stage, Close-Up, Mentalism and Junior categories, the International Gala Show with world-class artists, lectures and workshops led by international masters, and a magic shop with dealers from all over the world.

For the audience

International Gala Show

The highlight of the festival: the most acclaimed artists of the edition in a gala show for the whole family. The International Gala Show takes place at the “Elisabeta Bostan” House of Culture in Buhuși, Bacău, on the weekend of 7-8 May 2027. Early Bird tickets start at 69 lei (Standard), 109 lei (Premium) and 149 lei (VIP) and are sold separately from the registration packages - magicians registered with a Standard or Full package already have access included. At the first edition, in February 2026, the gala brought magicians from 6 countries and 3 continents to the stage in Buhuși.
