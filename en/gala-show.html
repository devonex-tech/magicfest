The Main Event

International Magic Gala Show 2027 in Romania

The MagicArt Fest gala: an evening of world-class illusion where the impossible becomes reality, on the stage of the “Elisabeta Bostan” House of Culture in Buhuși.

MAY 2027

A show unique in Romania

Close your MagicArt Fest experience alongside the festival's most acclaimed artists, in a spectacular gala show for the whole family.

The same stage will host internationally renowned magicians, laureates of the world's most important magic competitions, presenting striking illusions, comedy moments, manipulation, mentalism and outstanding visual effects.

The Gala Show is the highlight of the festival - a high-quality production full of emotion, surprise and memorable moments, created for audiences of all ages. Book your seat and live a magical experience you will never forget!

When May 7-8, 2027
Location “Elisabeta Bostan” House of Culture, Buhuși
Age Recommended 6+
Price From 69 Lei - Early Bird price!
Buy Tickets for the International Gala Show

What you see at the magic gala show

Inside the show

  • Large-scale stage illusions and visual effects.
  • Manipulation and card magic, performed in plain sight.
  • Magic comedy, for audiences of every age.
  • Mentalism: predictions and high-impact psychological effects.

Practical information

  • Dates: 7-8 May 2027, the 2nd edition of MagicArt Fest.
  • Venue: "Elisabeta Bostan" House of Culture, Buhuși, Bacău county, Romania.
  • Recommended age: 6 and over.
  • Every ticket includes seating in the hall and access to the Magic Shop.

The magicians on the 2026 gala stage

At the first edition, held on 5-7 February 2026 in Buhuși, the International Gala Show stage welcomed Jeki Yoo (South Korea/USA), Gabriel Gascón (Chile), Kimoon Do (South Korea), Piksi (Serbia), Eduard and Bianca (Romania), Teatro Blu Magic (Romania) and Christianis, the founder of the festival.

See the 2026 guests of honour or browse the 2026 photo and video gallery.

Tickets for the international gala

Tickets come in three versions at Early Bird prices: Standard 69 lei (full price 89 lei), Premium 109 lei (full price 129 lei) and VIP 149 lei (full price 169 lei). The VIP ticket includes a seat in the front rows, priority access to the hall, a meet and greet with photos after the show and a MagicArt Fest surprise gift.

The magic competition inside the festival

The international magic competition runs on the same days as the gala: a FISM Qualified Contest with Stage, Close-Up, Mentalism and Junior categories, judged by an international jury.

Read the magic competition rules. If you are a magician, register for MagicArt Fest 2027: the Standard package costs 119 euro, the Full package 149 euro, and access to the International Gala Show is included in both.
