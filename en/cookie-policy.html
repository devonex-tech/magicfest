Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

magicartfest.eu uses very few cookies. The strictly necessary ones keep the site running. The analytics ones are not loaded at all until you press "Accept all" or switch on the analytics category in the preferences panel. You can change your choice at any time.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that your browser saves on your device when you visit a website. They let the site recognise you on a later visit, or count visits correctly. Alongside cookies, websites can use similar technologies such as localStorage, which keeps information directly in the browser without sending it with every request.

2. The categories we use

2.1. Strictly necessary (always on)

These make the site work: moving between pages, the registration form, the payment process and remembering your cookie choice. Without them the site cannot work properly, so they cannot be switched off. They are not used for tracking and they do not build profiles.

2.2. Analytics and statistics (off by default)

These show us how many people visit the site, which pages they read and where they run into trouble, so we can improve the experience. We use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity. These tools load only after your explicit acceptance. If you decline, the scripts are not downloaded and their cookies are never created.

We do not use advertising, remarketing or commercial profiling cookies. Google Consent Mode v2 is configured on this site with ad_storage, ad_user_data and ad_personalization permanently denied.

3.3. Embedded third-party content

Some pages include a Google Maps frame or videos from YouTube. When such an embed loads, that provider may set its own cookies under its own policies. YouTube videos are embedded, where possible, through the privacy-enhanced domain youtube-nocookie.com. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which does not set tracking cookies.

4. How to change your choice

You can reopen the preferences panel at any time: . From there you can switch the analytics category on or off and save your new choice. If you withdraw consent, we stop sending new data to Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity from that moment.

You can also delete or block cookies directly in your browser. Instructions are in the help sections of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, Microsoft Edge or Opera. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may break parts of the site.

5. What happens to the data collected

Statistical data is used solely for aggregate analysis. We do not sell it, we do not combine it with your registration data in order to identify you, and we do not use it for advertising. Details on legal bases, recipients, international transfers and your rights are in our Privacy Policy.

6. Changes

If we add or remove tools, we update the tables above and the date at the top of the page. Where a change involves a new category of cookies, we will ask for your consent again.
